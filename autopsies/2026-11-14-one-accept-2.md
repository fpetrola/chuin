## Symptom

## Cause

A group of radio buttons had its options behind a door of their own, acceptOptions with an OptionVisitor, next to the accept that only announced the group. The painter opened that door and walked the options itself, so the knowledge that a group is made of options, and which one is chosen, sat in whoever visited it instead of in the group's own accept. Each option also travelled as a bare string, with nothing to say which group it came from.
model: structure-walked-from-outside

## Principle

Only the element knows its structure: a group's single accept visits the group and then has the visitor meet each option, each carrying the group it belongs to, so no visitor decides to walk the options or needs to remember which group they came from.
