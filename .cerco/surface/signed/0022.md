## Need

ClickingWithTheMouseTest needs a left click on a button to focus and press it, and on a check box to focus and toggle it, the way Turbo Vision answers the mouse. Terminals report the mouse as ESC [ < button ; column ; row M (pressed) or m (released); the keyboard must hand a left press over with the screen cell it happened on, which no sealed key can carry today.

## Considered

KeyVisitor#other() is what a mouse report turns into today and drops where it happened; KeyVisitor#enter() and KeyVisitor#space() press and toggle only the view that already has the focus, not the one under the pointer. Releases and other buttons stay other() until a test needs them, so one member is enough.
