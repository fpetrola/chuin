## Need

DrawingAWindowTest needs a desktop to draw its open windows on an in-memory screen: a window placed on an area of the desktop, with a framed centred title, and a button placed at a position inside it drawn as its caption between brackets. That needs geometry in cells (Point, Area) a renderer can read, a Screen it can write text on and read back as lines, and a ViewVisitor that hands the renderer each window's title and area, each child's position and each button's caption, so drawing happens in a visitor and no class grows methods of its own.

## Considered

ViewVisitor#window(List<Button>) hands over the buttons but not the title, the area or where each button sits, and ViewVisitor#button(Button, List<ButtonListener>) does not hand over the caption, so a renderer visiting a window has nothing to draw. Window#add(Button) gives a button no position.

## Removes

- `com.fpetrola.tui.ViewVisitor#window(List<Button>): void`, replaced by window(String, Area) plus child(Point) before each placed view
- `com.fpetrola.tui.ViewVisitor#button(Button, List<ButtonListener>): void`, replaced by button(Button, String, List<ButtonListener>)
