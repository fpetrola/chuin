## Need

Steps 8 and 9 of splitting chuin-text into packages under com.fpetrola.chuin.text, done as one (PLAN.md, "Paquetes de chuin-text"). After this the root com.fpetrola.chuin holds only the API: no chuin-text class is left in it, so dependencies point one way, from text.* to the API.
The menu bar, the menus, the status line and what places and draws their entries move to com.fpetrola.chuin.text.menu, with MenuDesktop, the desktop that puts them over the windows. The classes that waited at the root move too: TextToolkit to com.fpetrola.chuin.text, StreamKeyboard to text.input, and ShownDesktop and TextConsole to text.desktop. They implement or use Desktop, Keyboard and Console from the API root, so they could not leave the root while chuin-text classes stayed in it. MenuDesktop could not leave before TextToolkit, which builds it; moving them together keeps the menu's own painters and placers package-private, instead of making five of their constructors public for one step.
TextToolkit builds every part, so these become public: the constructors TextMenu(String), TextMenuBar(), TextStatusLine(), MenuDesktop(Windows), StreamKeyboard(InputStream, Toolkit) and TextConsole(Keyboard, Terminal, OutputStream, Screen). TextMenuBar() and TextStatusLine() were implicit; they are now written out so that they are signed like any other entrance. TextToolkit() and TextToolkit(Map) are the same constructors under the new package. Everything else in text.menu stays package-private: EntryPlacer, EntryRow, PlacedEntry, PlacedTitle and their implementations, KeyLabel, KeyCaption, MenuPainter, StatusPainter, StatusClick, TextMenuItem and TextStatusItem, and so does ShownDesktop in text.desktop.
The demo's imports change from com.fpetrola.chuin.TextToolkit to com.fpetrola.chuin.text.TextToolkit, as do the tests'. AssemblingTheToolkitTest, new, builds each of the new public parts from outside its package, as TextToolkit does. PlacingATitleOnTheBarTest, PuttingEntriesInARowTest and ReadingAMarkedTextTest move to text.menu. Nothing changes in behaviour or signature, only the package and the visibility.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.chuin.Toolkit#menuBar(): MenuBar` and the other factories of the sealed Toolkit make every part for an application, but the toolkit that implements them sat in the API's root package. The EntryPlacer, EntryRow, PlacedEntry, PlacedTitle and TextToolkit sealed so far (taken back under Removes) said what the new ones say, but in the flat package at the root.
