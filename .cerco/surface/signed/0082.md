## Need

Step 2 of splitting chuin-text into packages under com.fpetrola.chuin.text (PLAN.md, "Paquetes de chuin-text"): what draws on a screen and shows it on a terminal moves to com.fpetrola.chuin.text.screen. That is TextScreen, ClippedScreen, AnsiTerminal, Palette, DosPalette, Tint and TintRole. The rest of the module tints cells and writes on screens from other packages, so Tint and Palette, with their members, become public. So do the constructors the rest of the module calls: TextScreen(int, int) and AnsiTerminal(OutputStream, Palette) from TextToolkit, and ClippedScreen(Screen, Area) from Painter. Nothing changes in behaviour or signature, only the package and the visibility. No test named those three constructors, because the tests reach them through TextToolkit; MakingScreensTest, new, makes each one from outside its package and says what it promises. TintingCellsTest moves to text.screen beside Tint.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.chuin.screen.Screen#paint(int, int, int, char): void` in chuin-api paints cells with a bare letter, but it does not say what a tint is for or how a terminal colours it. That is what Tint and Palette say. The Tint and Palette sealed so far (com.fpetrola.chuin.Tint and com.fpetrola.chuin.Palette, taken back under Removes) said it too, but package-private in the flat package, which the painters in text.paint and text.menu could not reach.
