## Need

Second step of the classic visitor (DECISIONS 28): ViewVisitor#listBox handed every visitor seven loose values, the items, rows, top, selection and two Runnables, taken out of the list box. The visitor must receive only the ListBox, and the list must answer for itself; its rows and scroll bar are walked by the list through a nested ListRowVisitor, as Mark is, so the painter never holds the list's items, and cerco's handed-out-whole rule rejects handing them out.

## Considered

ViewVisitor#listBox(ListBox, List<String>, int, int, int, Runnable, Runnable) is what this replaces. Getters for items, rows, top and selection were tried first: cerco flagged items() as handed-out-whole, and they would leave the scroll bar arithmetic in the painter, outside the object that knows the selection. ListBox#selected() stays for the application.
