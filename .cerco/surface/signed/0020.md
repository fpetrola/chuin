## Need

ChoosingARadioButtonTest needs a group of options of which exactly one is chosen, drawn one per row as (•) for the chosen one and ( ) for the rest, the way Turbo Vision draws its radio buttons. The group is one focus stop; while it has the focus the up and down arrows choose the option before or after, stopping at both ends. Toolkit must build it and the view visitor must hand over its options, the chosen one, and how to move the choice.

## Considered

ViewVisitor#checkBox(CheckBox, String, Mark, Runnable) holds one independent yes/no, and a row of check boxes cannot keep exactly one chosen. ViewVisitor#listBox(ListBox, List<String>, int, int, int, Runnable, Runnable) keeps one selection but scrolls and draws a scroll bar, and its selection is a highlight, not a choice shown as (•); radio buttons never scroll, so they hand over no rows or top.
