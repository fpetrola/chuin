## Grows

  - `com.fpetrola.tui.ListBox#pageUp(): void`
  - `com.fpetrola.tui.ListBox#pageDown(): void`

## Need

A click on the scroll bar's track above or below the thumb moves the selection a page, as in Turbo Vision. How far a page goes depends on how many rows the list shows, which only the list knows. Without these members the click had to count the bar cells it was handed and call next() that many times, rebuilding a private count from the visit order (cerco-tdd-97 caught it, DECISIONS 55).

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.ListBox#next(): void` and `com.fpetrola.tui.ListBox#previous(): void` move one item; a page would need the caller to know the rows shown, which ListBox does not tell and should not.
