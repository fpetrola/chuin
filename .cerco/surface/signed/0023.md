## Grows

- tui-api
  - `com.fpetrola.tui.Desktop#click(Point): void`

## Need

ClickingWithTheMouseTest needs a left click to focus the view under the pointer and press a button or toggle a check box, the way Turbo Vision answers the mouse. Terminals report the mouse inside the same byte stream as the keys (ESC [ < button ; column ; row M), so the reader of that stream must hand a left press to the desktop together with the screen cell it happened on.

## Considered

KeyVisitor#click(int, int), signed in 0022, carried the click as if it were a key; Fernando pointed out that a click is not a key, and that the name would lie more as dragging, the wheel and releases arrive. Desktop#type(Key) is for keys only. Desktop is what answers the click, and Point is already sealed for a screen cell, so the click enters through Desktop#click(Point).

## Removes

- `com.fpetrola.tui.KeyVisitor#click(int, int): void`
