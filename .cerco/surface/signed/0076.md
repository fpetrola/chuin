## Need

Where each entry of a row goes, each starting where the one before ends, was worked out three times (plan step B3): TextEntryRow moved its column on past each entry it drew, StatusClick added up the same widths to find the status entry under a click, and MenuDesktop's placed() added them up again to place the menu titles; and whether a column falls on an entry was written twice, in StatusClick and in TextPlacedTitle#covers. EntryPlacer#place(Caption) puts the next entry where the row is and moves on past it, and the PlacedEntry it gives knows its start() and whether it covers(int) a column. TextEntryRow draws each entry at the start its placer gives, StatusClick runs the item whose placed entry covers the click, and MenuDesktop hands each title its placed entry, which TextPlacedTitle covers with on the top row and opens its menu at. EntryRow#next() goes, as nothing asks it any more. No behaviour changes.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.EntryRow#put(Caption, Tint, Tint): void` places and draws an entry, but needs a screen, which a click on the status line or on the bar does not have; `com.fpetrola.tui.PlacedTitle#covers(Point): boolean` covers a menu title only, while the status line's entries are placed the same way and had nothing.
