## Need

Step 4 of PLAN.md: menu titles, menu items, buttons and labels mark one letter between tildes, "~D~atei", as Turbo Vision does, and show it highlighted while the tildes are not drawn; "~~" is a tilde. Painters need the text as shown, for widths and drawing, and the place of the marked letter to paint it, so a marked text needs a type of its own that reads the marks once.

## Considered

Drawing#drawOn(Screen) draws a whole thing at a fixed place, but a caption is drawn inside a button face or a menu row whose position and width depend on the caption itself; Border#top(String, int) takes a plain label. Parsing tildes in each painter would repeat the same rule in MenuPainter, Painter and TextMenu.
