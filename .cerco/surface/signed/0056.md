## Need

MenuTitles answered every question about the bar by index: menu(int), start(int), rows(int), items(int), and titleAt/answering returned an index that the Opened state kept and the key visitors passed back. plural-name froze it, and Fernando and cerco-tdd-97 asked for one object per title. PlacedTitle is a menu's title where the bar puts it: it knows its menu, whether a cell falls on it, whether it answers a letter, which item of its open menu is drawn at a cell, and its items. MenuDesktop walks the bar and places them; the Opened state holds the title itself. No behaviour changes; the existing tests are untouched.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.Menu#title(): String` and `com.fpetrola.tui.Menu#width(): int` give a menu's caption and box width, but not where the bar puts its title, so they cannot say whether a cell is on it or which item is drawn there. `com.fpetrola.tui.MenuTitles#titleAt(Point): OptionalInt` did, by index, which is what this replaces.
