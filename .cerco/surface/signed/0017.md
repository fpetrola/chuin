  - `com.fpetrola.tui.Toolkit#listBox(List<String>, int): ListBox`

## Need

ScrollingAListBoxTest needs a list of items a window can hold that shows only some rows at a time, keeps a selection the arrow keys move while it has the focus, scrolls to keep the selection in sight, and draws a scroll bar beside the rows with a thumb where the selection is. Toolkit must build it and the view visitor must hand over its items, rows, first row shown, selection, and how to select the one before or after, so the desktop and the painter work on it without casts.

## Considered

ViewVisitor#label(String) shows one line of text and nothing can be selected in it; stacking labels cannot scroll or keep a selection. KeyVisitor#up() and KeyVisitor#down() already exist and move the top window; with a list focused they select instead, so no key grows.
