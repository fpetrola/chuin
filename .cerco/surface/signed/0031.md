  - `com.fpetrola.tui.RadioButtons#choose(int): void`

## Need

ClickingWithTheMouseTest needs a left click on a row of a list box to select the item shown there, and a click on an option to choose it, the way Turbo Vision answers the mouse; today a click there only focuses the view (DECISIONS 26). The desktop knows which row of the view was clicked from where it was drawn, so the list and the group must take a choice by place: the row among those shown, and the option among the group's.

## Considered

ListBox#previous(), ListBox#next(), RadioButtons#previous() and RadioButtons#next() move one step at a time; reaching a clicked row with them would mean the desktop knowing the selection and counting steps, which is the list's own knowledge. ListBox#selected() only reads the item.
