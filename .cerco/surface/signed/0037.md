## Need

Fourth step of one accept per element (DECISIONS 39): a group of radio buttons offered a second way in, acceptOptions(OptionVisitor), so the painter chose to walk its options and had to know the group has them; cerco's one-accept rule froze it. RadioButtons#accept must visit the group and then each option as visits of the same ViewVisitor, each option an Option that knows its group and text, so no visitor has to remember which group it is in.

## Considered

ViewVisitor#visitRadioButtons(RadioButtons) already meets the group, and RadioButtons#width(), RadioButtons#previous(), RadioButtons#next() and RadioButtons#choose(int) stay. ListItem from 0036 also has a list and a text, but it belongs to a ListBox; an option belongs to a group, so it gets its own small Option, as cerco-tdd-97 agreed for this step.
