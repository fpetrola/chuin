## Grows

  - `com.fpetrola.tui.ListItem#select(): void`

## Need

kept-by-index froze TextRadioButtons#choose(int) and TextListBox#selectShown(int): a click or a letter counted the options or rows it visited and handed the position back to the group or the list. The options and rows are already handed out to visitors as Option and ListItem, but they could not act. With Option#choose() and ListItem#select() the element a person points at chooses itself, like DropDownItem#choose, MenuItem#run and StatusItem#run; the int methods go away. AtFocus now also passes a focused group's options and a focused list's rows on to the action. No behaviour changes; the click and letter tests are untouched.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.RadioButtons#choose(int): void` and `com.fpetrola.tui.ListBox#selectShown(int): void` do it by position, which is what kept-by-index marks and this replaces; `com.fpetrola.tui.RadioButtons#next(): void` and `com.fpetrola.tui.ListBox#next(): void` move one step and cannot reach a given element.

## Removes

- `com.fpetrola.tui.RadioButtons#choose(int): void`
- `com.fpetrola.tui.ListBox#selectShown(int): void`
