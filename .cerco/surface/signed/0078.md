## Need

Keeping the current one of a fixed number of places between the first and the last was written out with Math.max and Math.min wherever a selection moves (plan step B5): TextListBox five times (previous, next, pageUp, pageDown, moveTo), TextOptionGroup twice, and the open menu's up and down in MenuDesktop, each with its own idea of the ends (the option group's next had no lower bound, the menu's up no upper one). Cursor names it: Cursor#at() is the current place, Cursor#step(int) moves on or back stopping at either end, and Cursor#moveTo(int) goes to a place within them. The list's selection, the group's chosen option and the open menu's item are each a Cursor. The list box's four moves now go through one, which also keeps the selection in sight the same way moveTo did. TextDropDown's highlight (an Optional, empty while the list is closed) and a panel axis's position (kept by the panel, from 0 to most) are left for later, as they keep their place differently. No behaviour changes.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.Scrollable#moveTo(int): void` moves a scrollable view to a position between 0 and most, but it is what a scroll bar asks of the view, which still had to keep that position itself; `com.fpetrola.tui.FocusOrder#next(): boolean` counts places as a walk meets them, but does not keep one.
