## Need

Third step of one accept per element (DECISIONS 39): a list box offered a second way in, acceptRows(ListRowVisitor), so the painter chose to walk its rows and had to know the list has them, which cerco 0.26.0's one-accept rule froze. ListBox#accept must visit the box and then each row it shows and its scroll bar cell, as visits of the same ViewVisitor that each say which list they belong to (a ListItem knows its list and text; the other rows and the scroll bar cells get the ListBox alone, since passing the box together with a part is what visitor-handed-parts forbids), and a visitor that does not care about rows does nothing when it meets them.

## Considered

ViewVisitor#visitListBox(ListBox) already meets the box, and ListBox#selected(), ListBox#width(), ListBox#previous(), ListBox#next() and ListBox#selectShown(int) stay as they are; only the rows and scroll bar cells move from the list's own row visitor into the one ViewVisitor, with the same eight cases.
