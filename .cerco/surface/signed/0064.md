## Need

Fernando asked for a ScrollPanel that works for any component: a view of a given size that holds another view and shows part of it, with scroll bars when it does not fit. For that the panel has to know how big what it holds is, which only the painter knew, by measuring what it wrote. View#size() tells how many columns and rows a view takes where it is placed, as it is drawn now: a button its face and shadow, a label its text, a check box its box and text, an input line its width or its text, a list its width by its rows, a group its width by its options, a drop down its field and arrow, a scroll bar one column by its length. Size is that pair. The painter takes the drawn width of input lines, drop downs, lists and groups from it, so it has one owner.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.ListBox#width(): int`, `com.fpetrola.tui.InputLine#width(): int` and the other widths say how wide some views are, but not all views have one, none says its rows, and a view held by a panel is only known as View. `com.fpetrola.tui.Area#columns(): int` and rows() carry a size, but also a place, which a size has not.
