## Need

Step 4b of PLAN.md: Alt with a letter must reach what shows that letter highlighted, as in Turbo Vision: a menu title opens its menu, with a menu open the letter alone runs an item, a button is focused and pressed, and a label passes the focus to the view it is the label of. A caption must say whether it answers to a letter, and a label must be able to be linked to a view and say which one.

## Considered

Caption#highlight(Screen, int, int, char) paints the marked letter but does not say which one it is; a getter of the letter would let callers compare it themselves, while answers keeps the comparison, and its case rule, inside the caption. Toolkit#label(String) builds a label that stands alone and can reach nothing.
