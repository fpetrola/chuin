## Need

Step 4 of splitting chuin-text into packages under com.fpetrola.chuin.text (PLAN.md, "Paquetes de chuin-text"). The models of the views move to com.fpetrola.chuin.text.view: the button, label, check box, input line, list box, option group, drop down, scroll bar, scroll panel, window, group and placement, and the Cursor, Caption, Scrollable and PanelAxis they keep. None of them depends on what is left at the root. TextToolkit makes the views from the root, the painters and menus read captions, and the menu keeps a cursor, so these become public: the constructors of TextButton, TextLabel, TextCheckBox, TextInputLine, TextListBox, TextOptionGroup, TextDropDown, TextScrollPanel, TextWindow, TextCaption and TextCursor, and the Caption, Cursor and Group interfaces. Scrollable, PanelAxis, TextPlacement and TextScrollBar are used only inside text.view, so they stay package-private. MakingViewsTest, new, makes each view from outside its package and checks what it starts with. ScrollingWithABarTest and KeepingACursorAmongPlacesTest move to text.view. ReadingAMarkedTextTest stays at the root, because it also reads KeyCaption, which moves with the menus. Nothing changes in behaviour or signature, only the package and the visibility.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.chuin.Toolkit#button(String): Button` and the other factories of the sealed Toolkit make every view for an application, but TextToolkit, which implements them, has to reach the view classes from the root. The Caption, Cursor, Group and Scrollable sealed so far (taken back under Removes) said what the new ones say, but package-private in the flat package.
