## Need

SeeingAMenuBarTest needs a menu bar across the top row of the screen showing the titles of its menus, the way Turbo Vision's desktop has one over its windows. This is the first step of the menu bar; opening a menu with F10 and choosing an item come next. The toolkit must build a bar and its menus, the desktop must show a bar, and the bar must walk its menus so the desktop can draw their titles.

## Considered

Desktop#open(Window) could hold a window placed on the top row, but a menu bar is not a window: it has no frame, is always on top, and F10 and the mouse will reach it whichever window has the focus. ListBox#acceptRows(ListRowVisitor) shows how a view walks its insides; the bar walks its menus the same way.
