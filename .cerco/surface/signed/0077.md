## Grows

- tui-text
  - `com.fpetrola.tui.Extent interface`
  - `com.fpetrola.tui.Extent#covers(Point): boolean`
  - `com.fpetrola.tui.Extent#within(Area): Area`
  - `com.fpetrola.tui.Extent#from(Point): Point`

## Need

Whether a cell falls in an area was written out as column and row bounds four times, three in TextDesktop (the view under a click, the window under a click, whether the release is over the focused view) and once in ClippedScreen's cursor; the part of an area another shows was a static helper of Painter; and a cell counted from an area's corner was subtracted by hand three times in TextDesktop, for a click on the focused view, its drag and a click on an open list (plan step B4). Area, in tui-api, only says where an area is. Extent says what it spans: Extent#covers(Point) is whether a cell falls in it, Extent#within(Area) is its part another area shows, and Extent#from(Point) is a cell counted from its corner. TextExtent works it out for any Area. It stays in tui-text, as the plan says, until tui-api needs it. No behaviour changes.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.ClickTarget#click(Point, Runnable): void` finds what is under a cell for a bar or a view drawn, but each target still had to say whether the cell falls on it; `com.fpetrola.tui.BarLine#holds(Point): boolean` says it for a line of cells, not for an area.
