## Need

Fernando asked to reorganise the public interfaces into packages, and chose to do it in tui-api only: tui-text is almost all package-private, and splitting it would make many classes public. The interfaces are grouped by what they are, and the packages depend one way, which cerco checked when a first grouping made a cycle: geometry (Point, Area, Size) and input (Key, KeyVisitor) depend on nothing; screen (Screen, Terminal) and view (the views, ViewVisitor, Window, Placement, ScrollBar, ScrollPanel) on geometry; menu (menu bar, menus, status line) on input; and com.fpetrola.tui (Toolkit, Desktop, Keyboard, Console) on the rest. Every member is the same; only the packages change, so each type grows under its new name and goes away under the old. The four visitors become public, which they had to be all along: tui-text reached them only because both modules shared one package.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.Toolkit#desktop(): Desktop` stays where it is, with Desktop, Keyboard and Console, at the top that depends on the rest; the other types keep every member as signed, and only their names change, which a seal cannot say without growing them under the new name.
