## Need

Step C of scrolling a window: a click on the scroll bars of its frame scrolls it, ▲▼◄► a cell and the track a page toward the click, never past what it holds (cerco-tdd-7c asked for that limit). Only the bars know how much the window holds and where the thumb is, from what was drawn through them, so they take the click: ClickTarget#click(Point, Runnable otherwise) does what a click on that cell does, or runs otherwise when it does not fall there, as Windows#escape(Runnable) does. Each bar, right and bottom, is a target of its own. The desktop keeps the top window's targets from its last draw, as it keeps its views' areas, and chains them: each gets the next as otherwise, and the last the click on the window.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.Drawing#drawOn(Screen): void` is what the bars already are, and it only draws; `com.fpetrola.tui.Windows#escape(Runnable): void` has this shape, but for a key, not a cell; `com.fpetrola.tui.Windows#click(Point): void` is the desktop's own click, with nothing to run when a part of the frame does not take it.
