## Need

DraggingAWindowTest needs a window to follow the pointer while its title is dragged, as in Turbo Vision, the first step of making windows, scroll bars and drop downs work with the mouse. Terminals report a drag as a press, moves with the left button held and a release; the keyboard must hand the moves and the release to the desktop, and the desktop and its windows must take them.

## Considered

Desktop#click(Point) and Windows#click(Point) take only the press of the left button; a move with the button held and the release come as other keys today, so nothing can follow the pointer. KeyVisitor#up() and the other arrows move a window one cell per key, not with the pointer.
