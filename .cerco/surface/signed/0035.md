## Grows

- tui-api
  - `com.fpetrola.tui.ViewVisitor#visitChecked(CheckBox): void`
  - `com.fpetrola.tui.ViewVisitor#visitUnchecked(CheckBox): void`

## Need

Second step of one accept per element (DECISIONS 39): a check box offered a second way in, acceptMark(MarkVisitor), with a visitor of its own, so the painter chose to walk the mark and had to know the box has one. CheckBox#accept must visit the box and then its mark itself, as visitChecked or visitUnchecked on the same ViewVisitor, so the state is still told without a flag and a visitor that does not care about the mark ignores it.

## Considered

CheckBox#toggle() and CheckBox#text() stay as they are. ViewVisitor#visitCheckBox(CheckBox) already meets the box, but alone it cannot tell the mark without a boolean, which Fernando ruled out when the check box was made, so the mark becomes two more visits of the same visitor instead of a second visitor.

## Removes

- `com.fpetrola.tui.CheckBox#acceptMark(MarkVisitor): void`
- `com.fpetrola.tui.MarkVisitor interface`
- `com.fpetrola.tui.MarkVisitor#checked(): void`
- `com.fpetrola.tui.MarkVisitor#unchecked(): void`
