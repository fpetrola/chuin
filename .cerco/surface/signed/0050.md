## Need

Step 6b of PLAN.md: Quadra has a drop down ("↓Ja↓"), a field showing one chosen item of a list with an arrow, whose list opens under it to choose another: ↓ opens it with the chosen item highlighted, the arrows walk it, Enter chooses and closes, Esc closes keeping the choice. The toolkit must build one, it must take those moves, say what is chosen, and be visited with its items while open so it can be drawn over everything, past its window if need be. While a list is open, Escape must close it before any binding, as with an open menu, so the windows must take Escape first and say when nothing was open.

## Considered

ListBox#selectShown(int), ListBox#previous() and ListBox#next() keep a selection in a list that is always shown in its window and scrolls; a drop down shows one item and opens over the views under it, closing again, and its highlighted item is not chosen until Enter. RadioButtons#choose(int) shows every option all the time. Windows#type(Key) hands a key to the windows but cannot tell the menu desktop that nothing took it, which Escape needs to fall back to its binding.
