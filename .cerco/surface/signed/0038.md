## Need

Last step of one accept per element (DECISIONS 39): the menus were walked from outside, MenuBar#acceptMenus by the desktop and Menu#acceptItems by the painter, each with a visitor of its own, so whoever drew or ran a menu had to know a bar has menus and a menu has items. MenuBar#accept must visit the bar and then each menu, and Menu#accept the menu and then each item, as a MenuItem that knows its menu, its text and how to run itself; visitors ignore what they do not need.

## Considered

MenuVisitor#visitMenu(Menu) stays and is joined by the bar and its items, so a menu bar has one visitor as views have ViewVisitor. Menu#add(String, Runnable) and Menu#title() stay; Menu#width() moves the box's width onto the menu that knows its items, as ListBox#width() did for lists.
