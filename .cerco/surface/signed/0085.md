## Need

Step 5 of splitting chuin-text into packages under com.fpetrola.chuin.text (PLAN.md, "Paquetes de chuin-text"). What a click and a drag fall on, and what is done once a walk of the views is over, move to com.fpetrola.chuin.text.pointer: ClickTarget, Grab, Dragging, BarTarget, BarClick, BarLine, TextBarLine, Settle and PendingAction. They depend only on the API, text.geometry and nothing at the root. Painter, RowPainter and BarCells (going to text.paint) and TextDesktop and the focus actions (going to text.desktop) use them from the root, so all these become public. Their four constructors become public too: TextBarLine(Point, Point), BarTarget(ScrollBar, BarLine, int, Grab), BarClick(int, Settle, BarLine, Grab) and PendingAction(Runnable). Moving this package out first also breaks the cycle between paint and desktop that the plan found: the painter needs click targets and grabs, and the desktop needs the painter. ClickingOnABarTest, new, makes a BarClick from outside its package, as a list's click does. LayingAScrollBarTest and SettlingAfterAVisitTest move to text.pointer. Nothing changes in behaviour or signature, only the package and the visibility.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.chuin.Desktop#click(Point): void` and `com.fpetrola.chuin.Desktop#drag(Point): void` in chuin-api take a click and a drag for the whole desktop, but they do not say which drawn thing a click falls on, how a scroll bar's cells lie, or who follows a drag. The ClickTarget, Grab, Dragging, BarLine and Settle sealed so far (taken back under Removes) said it, but package-private in the flat package.
