## Grows

- tui-text
  - `com.fpetrola.tui.Caption#entryWidth(): int`

## Need

How wide a caption is as an entry of a row, its shown text and a blank on each side, was worked out in four places: TextEntryRow when it moves past an entry, StatusClick when it finds the entry under a click, and MenuDesktop and TextPlacedTitle when they place a menu's title and ask whether a cell falls on it. Each wrote `shown().length() + 2` on its own, so a change in how entries are spaced would draw them in one place and click them in another. Caption#entryWidth() says it once; the four ask the caption. No behaviour changes.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.Caption#shown(): String` is the text without the blanks, so every caller had to add them itself; `com.fpetrola.tui.EntryRow#next(): int` is where the next entry starts on a row being drawn, but the click on the status line and the titles placed for the mouse do not draw a row.
