## Need

Fourth step of the classic visitor (DECISIONS 28): ViewVisitor#checkBox handed every visitor the box's text, its Mark and a toggle Runnable next to the box, which cerco 0.25.0's visitor-handed-parts rule froze. The visitor must receive only the CheckBox, and the box answers for its text, toggles itself, and tells its mark through the MarkVisitor it already had, so neither the painter nor the key actions hold its parts.

## Considered

ViewVisitor#checkBox(CheckBox, String, Mark, Runnable) is what this replaces. MarkVisitor#checked() and MarkVisitor#unchecked() already tell the state without a flag, as Fernando asked when the check box was made, so CheckBox#acceptMark(MarkVisitor) reuses them instead of a boolean getter or a Mark getter.

## Removes

- `com.fpetrola.tui.Mark interface`
- `com.fpetrola.tui.Mark#accept(MarkVisitor): void`
