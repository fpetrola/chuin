## Need

Frame, Shadow and RaisedFrame draw the same border and shadow for windows and open menus, so the painters share them instead of drawing by hand. To pass unpromised-member they implemented java.util.function.Consumer<Screen>, which met the rule's letter while bringing in a concept nobody designed or signed; Fernando caught it. The concept, something drawn the same way wherever it is needed, gets its own interface so it goes through a proposal like the rest.

## Considered

TextToolkit#<init>() is all tui-text seals, and nothing sealed there or in tui-api names a drawing: Screen#write(int, int, String) and Screen#paint(int, int, int, char) are what a Drawing uses, one cell row at a time, not something that can be passed around and drawn as a whole.
