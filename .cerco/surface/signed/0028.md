## Need

Fifth step of the classic visitor (DECISIONS 28): ViewVisitor#button handed visitors the caption and a copy of the listeners next to the button, so Enter and a mouse click each notified the listeners themselves, and ViewVisitor#label handed only the text, without the label. Both must be visited as themselves: Button answers for its caption and presses itself, Label answers for its text.

## Considered

ViewVisitor#button(Button, String, List<ButtonListener>) and ViewVisitor#label(String) are what this replaces. Button#onPress(ButtonListener) only lets listeners in and says nothing about notifying them, and Label#text(String) only sets the text; the getter shares its name, as the application already reads InputLine#text().
