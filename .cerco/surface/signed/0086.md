## Need

Step 6 of splitting chuin-text into packages under com.fpetrola.chuin.text (PLAN.md, "Paquetes de chuin-text"). What draws the windows and their views moves to com.fpetrola.chuin.text.paint: Painter, RowPainter, BarCells, FocusOrder, TextFocusOrder, Border, Line, Frame, RaisedFrame, Shadow, Drawing and DropDownList. They depend only on the API and on text.geometry, text.screen, text.view and text.pointer, not on the root. From the root, TextDesktop makes a Painter for each window with a Line and keeps the Drawing popups, AtFocus counts the focus with a TextFocusOrder, and the menus draw a RaisedFrame. So these become public: Border, Line, Drawing, FocusOrder, and the constructors of Painter, TextFocusOrder and RaisedFrame. RowPainter, BarCells, Frame, Shadow and DropDownList are used only inside text.paint, so they stay package-private. PaintingAWindowTest, new, makes a Painter from outside its package and checks the window it draws and where its focusable view went. CountingTheFocusOrderTest and DrawingFramesAndShadowsTest move to text.paint. Nothing changes in behaviour or signature, only the package and the visibility.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.chuin.Desktop#draw(Screen): void` in chuin-api draws the whole desktop, but it does not say how a window and its views are drawn, with which lines, or how the focus is counted on the way. The Border, Drawing and FocusOrder sealed so far (taken back under Removes) said it, but package-private in the flat package.
