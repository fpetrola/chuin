## Need

Fernando asked for a ScrollPanel that works for any component, and the window to use it so there is one way to scroll. ScrollPanel is a view of a size of its own that holds any view and shows part of it from an origin; its last column carries a ScrollBar for rows and its last row one for columns. Toolkit#scrollPanel(View, columns, rows) makes one. Visiting it enters it, goes on to what it holds and leaves it (visitScrollPanel, leaveScrollPanel), so the painter draws what it holds shifted and cut, then its bars. It knows how much it holds from View#size(); for a group of placed views, from Placement#area(), where a view sits and how big it is. A button's shadow is no longer part of its size, which changes what 0064 signed: with it a button on a window's last row made a panel think it held a row more and gave a false bar; the shadow falls on what is around it, as a window's does, and takes no room, so a button is its face. A Group, in tui-text, is the views placed together, the only owner of its placements; a window's inside is now a panel around its group, one column and row bigger than the inside so its bars fall on the frame; Window#origin() and Window#scroll() go away, and the measuring by writing (ScrollBars) with them. Bringing the focused view into sight scrolls the window's panel, with the view's size from its placement instead of the last drawing.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.ScrollBar#next(): void` and the other moves scroll what a bar is beside, but something has to hold the origin, the size and the two bars around any view; `com.fpetrola.tui.Placement#position(): Point` tells where a view sits but not how far it reaches, which a group needs for its size.
