## Need

Third step of the classic visitor (DECISIONS 28 and 29): ViewVisitor#radioButtons handed every visitor the group's options, the chosen index and two Runnables taken out of it. The visitor must receive only the RadioButtons; the group walks its own options through a nested OptionVisitor that tells the chosen one apart, and answers for its width and for moving the choice, so no visitor holds its list of options.

## Considered

ViewVisitor#radioButtons(RadioButtons, List<String>, int, Runnable, Runnable) is what this replaces. ListRowVisitor#item(String) and ListRowVisitor#selectedItem(String) walk rows too, but they come with a scroll bar cell per row that a group of options never has, so the group gets its own two-method OptionVisitor instead of sharing the list's.
