## Need

RadioButtons is named in plural after Turbo Vision's widget look; plural-name froze it, and Fernando asked to rename it. What it is in the model is a group of options of which exactly one is chosen, as its own javadoc says, and Option already chooses itself (0057). OptionGroup names the role of the whole. This proposal is the rename only: same members, same behaviour; the toolkit's factory and the visitor's method follow the new name, and TextRadioButtons becomes TextOptionGroup.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.Option#choose(): void` already makes an option the chosen one of its group, but the group it chooses in still has to be a sealed type, and that type was RadioButtons, the plural that plural-name keeps frozen until the type is renamed; there is no sealed name for the group other than the one being replaced.
