## Need

A click on an item of a drop down's open list should choose that item, as in Turbo Vision. The sealed DropDown only chooses the highlighted item, so a click would have to move the highlight with down() step by step, counting. The item knows its place: DropDownItem#choose(), like MenuItem#run and StatusItem#run.

Settle#note(Runnable): what a click or Esc does is decided while the views are walked and done once the walk is over. The desktop creates that pending action (PendingAction), hands it to the visitor (ClickFocused, ChooseFromOpenList, CloseOpenList), which notes into it, and settles it after the walk. note has callers outside the object that owns it: the three visitors; settle has TextDesktop. Before, each visitor kept its own due and ran it, a clone cerco caught; an earlier version of this proposal promoted a protected helper instead, which cerco-tdd-97 refused as DECISIONS 55.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.DropDown#choose(): void` chooses only the highlighted item; reaching another means repeated `com.fpetrola.tui.DropDown#down(): void` calls counted by the caller. `com.fpetrola.tui.Settle#settle(): void` runs what is due but gives no way to say what that is.
