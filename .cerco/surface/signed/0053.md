## Need

MenuDesktop is at 398 of 400 lines and resize is next. The bar's geometry (which menu sits where, which title is under a cell or answers a letter, a menu's rows and items) lived as private helpers inside MenuDesktop, shared by the Closed and Opened states and the key visitors. It is its own concept: the layout of the titles on the top row. Moving it out needs a type the desktop asks; no behaviour changes and every test stays as it is.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.MenuBar#accept(MenuVisitor)`: walking the bar gives each menu and item in order, but not where a title starts on the row, which one a cell falls on, or which answers a letter; every caller would redo that counting. `com.fpetrola.tui.Caption#answers(char)` answers for one caption, not for the bar.
