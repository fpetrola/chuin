## Need

Colours were sent only as exact RGB (38;2 and 48;2), which needs a terminal with 24-bit colour; the Linux console, older Terminal.app and tmux without it show them wrong. DECISIONS 59 left the fallback pending. A Palette gives the colours each tint is shown in; DosPalette has the DOS palette as RGB and as the 16 named colours (30-37, 40-47 and the bright 90-97). The toolkit picks RGB when COLORTERM is truecolor or 24bit and the named ones otherwise, reading the system's environment, or the one it is given: TextToolkit(Map) is how tests, and a program that knows better, say what terminal it is.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.Toolkit#terminal(OutputStream): Terminal` makes the terminal but says nothing about what it can show; `com.fpetrola.tui.Terminal#show(Screen): void` shows a screen in whatever colours it was made with.
