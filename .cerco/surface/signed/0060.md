## Need

Fernando asked for buttons that go down when pressed, as in Turbo Vision: pressing the mouse on a button holds it down, drawn a cell to the right without its shadow, and releasing over it presses it; releasing away lets it go unpressed. A button could only be pressed at once. Button#hold() and Button#letGo() keep whether the mouse holds it, and ViewVisitor#visitHeldButton(Button) tells a visitor, after visitButton, that it is held: the painter draws it down and the release (LetGo) lets it go and presses it. Keys still press at once.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.Button#press(): void` runs the listeners at once, with no state between the press of the mouse and its release, so nothing can be drawn down meanwhile nor can a release away cancel it.
