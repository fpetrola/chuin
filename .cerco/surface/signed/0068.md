## Need

What the desktop does with a drag had no name: the desktop kept a Consumer<Point> for moving and resizing windows, the bars of a panel got a Consumer<Consumer<Point>> to hand it a drag, and a list's bar a Consumer<IntConsumer>; cerco-tdd-7c asked for the concept to be named (DECISIONS 79). Dragging#to(Point) is what a drag does at each move of the pointer with the button held; Grab#hold(Dragging) is whoever follows the pointer taking a drag until the release. The desktop keeps a Dragging; the bars of a panel get a Grab in screen cells and a list's bar one in cells of the list, so both say the same thing. No behaviour changes.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.Windows#drag(Point): void` is the drag arriving at the windows, not what is done with it; `com.fpetrola.tui.ScrollBar#thumbTo(int): void` is one such thing, but a window moving or growing is another, and both are held the same way.
