## Grows

  - `com.fpetrola.tui.FocusOrder interface`

## Need

Which focusable view a walk is on, and whether it is the focused one, was counted by hand in AtFocus and in Painter, each with its own seen++ == focused (plan step B1). In Painter the count had already moved on by the time the check box highlighted its letter, so it read seen - 1 == focused, and the drop down counted with a bare seen++ whose meaning was nowhere said. FocusOrder#next() counts the focusable view just met and tells whether it is the focused one: AtFocus and Painter each walk with one, Painter's check box asks once and keeps the answer, and AtFocus's four copies of "count, route the parts, act" are one private step. CollectFocusable keeps deciding which views can take the focus, as it collects them; it counts nothing. No behaviour changes.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.Settle#note(Runnable): void` defers what the focused view does until the walk is over, but does not tell which view that is; `com.fpetrola.tui.ClickTarget#click(Point, Runnable): void` finds what is under a cell, not which view holds the focus.
