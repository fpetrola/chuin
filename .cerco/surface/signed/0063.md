## Need

Fernando asked for the scroll bars to be a component of their own, as Turbo Vision's TScrollBar, so any view that holds more than it shows can use one; first the list box, then a scroll panel the window will use. A list box drew its bar cell by cell, visited as ListBox. ScrollBar is a view that walks its own cells (▲, the track with its thumb, ▼, or blank cells) and whose moves scroll what it is beside; it has no place of its own. Scrollable, in tui-text, is what it is beside: where it is, how far it goes, whether it holds more than it shows, and the four moves, which TextListBox already had. The cell visits take the ScrollBar instead of the ListBox. A list box walks its rows and then its bar. No behaviour changes.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.ListBox#pageDown(): void` and the other moves of ListBox are what a bar needs to call, but only for a list: a window or a panel scrolls too, and a bar typed by ListBox cannot be beside them.
