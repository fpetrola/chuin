## Grows

- tui-api
  - `com.fpetrola.tui.Window#origin(): Point`
  - `com.fpetrola.tui.Window#scroll(int, int): void`

## Need

Fernando noticed that a window made smaller than what it holds cannot be navigated to what does not show. A window draws its views from its top left and cuts them at its frame, with no way to show another part. Window#origin() is the place of what it holds that shows at the top left inside its frame, and Window#scroll(int, int) moves it, never before 0, 0. The painter draws each view shifted by the origin; the desktop, when the focus goes to a view whose first cell is out of sight, scrolls the window just enough to show it. Scroll bars on the frame and the mouse on them come next, on these members.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.Window#place(Area): void` moves or resizes the window on the screen, but what shows inside stays tied to its top left: placing it elsewhere cannot show the part that is cut off.
