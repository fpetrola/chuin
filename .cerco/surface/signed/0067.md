## Need

Dragging the thumb of a scroll bar, as in Turbo Vision: pressing on the thumb takes it, and moving the pointer with the button held puts it on the cell under the pointer, what the bar is beside going as far along. The bar could only step and page. ScrollBar#thumbTo(int cell) puts the thumb on a cell of the bar, the ends of the track holding it; it asks what it is beside to go there with Scrollable#moveTo(int position), which a list answers by selecting that item and a panel by scrolling there. A press on a window's bar thumb hands the drag to the desktop, which already follows the pointer to move and resize windows.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.ScrollBar#next(): void` and `com.fpetrola.tui.ScrollBar#pageDown(): void` move by a step or a page; reaching the place under the pointer with them means counting steps from where it is.
