## Need

Last step of the classic visitor (DECISIONS 28): ViewVisitor#window handed visitors the title and area instead of the window, and the window then sent each child's position as a separate ViewVisitor#child call that every visitor had to track as state between calls. The window must be visited as itself, answer for its title and area, and walk its own children through a nested ChildVisitor that meets each view with its position; by default visiting a window visits its views.

## Considered

ViewVisitor#window(String, Area) and ViewVisitor#child(Point) are what this replaces; child(Point) only worked if every visitor kept the last position until the next view arrived, a protocol by convention. Window#place(Area) sets the area but nothing read it back, so MoveWindow had to wait for the visitor call to learn where the window was.

## Removes

- `com.fpetrola.tui.ViewVisitor#window(String, Area): void`
- `com.fpetrola.tui.ViewVisitor#child(Point): void`
