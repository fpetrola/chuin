## Need

A list box's scroll bar was drawn and clicked by code of its own: RowPainter wrote ▲ ░ ■ ▼ down the list's last column with its own counter, and ClickFocused walked the bar with its own pastThumb and counter, while the bars of a scroll panel did the same through BarCells and BarClick (plan step A1). What all four shared and had no name is where a bar lies: its first cell and the step to the next, down or across. BarLine says it: whether a cell is on the bar's line, how far along it a cell is, and which cell is a given count along. BarCells draws along a BarLine, BarClick takes the thumb's drag along it, BarTarget finds the clicked cell with it; RowPainter and ClickFocused now hand a list's bar to BarCells and BarClick on a BarLine down the list's last column. No behaviour changes.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.ClickTarget#click(Point, Runnable): void` is a click finding what was drawn under it, but a list's bar is clicked through the focused view, not as a target of its own; `com.fpetrola.tui.Grab#hold(Dragging): void` follows the drag, but says nothing of which cell along the bar the pointer is on.
