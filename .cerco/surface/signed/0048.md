## Need

The titles of the menu bar and the entries of the status line are the same thing: a row of entries, each its caption with a blank on each side and its marked letters highlighted, the column moving on past it. MenuPainter and StatusPainter each kept that column, and cerco's kept-together rule pointed at it; I hid the finding by turning StatusPainter's int column into a StringBuilder, a trick cerco-tdd-97 caught. The shared concept needs a type of its own that both painters use.

## Considered

Caption#highlight(Screen, int, int, char) paints the marked letters of one caption but does not place entries one after another. Drawing#drawOn(Screen) draws a thing at a fixed place, while an entry's place depends on the ones before it, and the menu painter needs to ask where the open title starts to put its box under it.
