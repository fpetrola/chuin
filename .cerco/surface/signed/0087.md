## Need

Step 7 of splitting chuin-text into packages under com.fpetrola.chuin.text (PLAN.md, "Paquetes de chuin-text"). The windows of the desktop, and what keys and clicks do to the focused view, move to com.fpetrola.chuin.text.desktop: TextDesktop, Windows, ToWindows, PointerWindows, AtFocus, CollectFocusable, the focus actions (PressFocused, ToggleFocused, TypeIntoFocused, EraseFocused, ClickFocused, ListArrow, ReachByLetter, ChooseFromOpenList, CloseOpenList, LetGo) and MoveWindow. They do not depend on the root. ShownDesktop and TextConsole stay at the root, like StreamKeyboard: they implement or use Desktop, Keyboard and Console from the API root, and moving them now makes a cycle. They move in the last step; PLAN.md says so. From the root, TextToolkit makes a TextDesktop behind a PointerWindows, and MenuDesktop hands keys on through a ToWindows. So Windows becomes public, and so do the constructors PointerWindows(Windows), ToWindows(Key, Windows) and TextDesktop(). TextDesktop() was implicit, so cerco did not see it; it is now written out, as TextToolkit() is, so that it is signed like any other entrance (note from the reviewer). AtFocus, CollectFocusable, the focus actions and MoveWindow are used only inside text.desktop, so they stay package-private. HandingOnToTheWindowsTest, new, makes a ToWindows, a PointerWindows and a TextDesktop from outside their package. DrivingTheWindowsTest and ClosingAnOpenListTest move to text.desktop. Nothing changes in behaviour or signature, only the package and the visibility.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.chuin.Desktop#type(Key): void` and the rest of the sealed Desktop in chuin-api take keys and clicks for the whole desktop, with its menu bar and status line, but say nothing of the windows alone, which the menus pass keys on to. The Windows sealed so far (taken back under Removes) said it, but package-private in the flat package.
