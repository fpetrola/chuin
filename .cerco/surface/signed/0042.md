## Need

Step 3 of PLAN.md: the escape key alone must close an open menu, and Alt with a letter will reach a view by its highlighted letter in step 4. Terminals send Escape as a lone ESC byte and Alt+letter as ESC before the letter, while arrows and function keys also start with ESC, so the keyboard must tell them apart by waiting a moment after ESC, and hand them over as keys of their own.

## Considered

KeyVisitor#other() is what a lone ESC or an ESC before a letter turns into today, so Escape and Alt+letter cannot be told from any other byte. KeyVisitor#control(char) carries Ctrl+letter, which terminals send as a single byte, not as a sequence; reusing it for Alt would make Ctrl+W and Alt+W the same key.
