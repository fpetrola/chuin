## Need

SeeingTheCursorTest needs the focused input line to show the terminal's cursor right after its text, and no cursor when the focus is on anything else, the way Turbo Vision tells which field takes typing now that a field keeps one colour focused or not. The screen must keep where the cursor goes or that it is hidden, so the painter can set it and the terminal can move and show it after drawing.

## Considered

Screen#paint(int, int, int, char) could tint the cursor cell, but a tint is a colour of the cell, not where typing goes, and the real terminal cursor blinks and is placed by the terminal itself. Screen#write(int, int, String) only puts characters.
