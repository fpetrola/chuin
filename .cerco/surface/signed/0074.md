  - `com.fpetrola.tui.PlacedTitle#drawOpen(Screen, int): void`

## Need

The open menu's box was laid out twice and had to agree (plan step B2): MenuPainter drew its items from row 2 + n at the title's column, rebuilding the menu's rows as the bar walked them, while TextPlacedTitle built the same rows again to find which item a click falls on, from row - ABOVE_ITEMS. Both knew where the box starts and how its rows stack; changing one would draw items where clicks do not find them. PlacedTitle already knew its menu, its column and its rows, so the box is now its to draw too: PlacedTitle#drawOpen(Screen, int) draws the frame, separators, items and keys at the same rows itemAt reads, and the menu desktop asks the open title to draw it after the bar. MenuPainter only draws the bar and its titles. A menu with no rows still shows no box. No behaviour changes.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.tui.PlacedTitle#itemAt(Point): Optional<MenuItem>` finds an item at a cell of the open box, but drawing it still had to repeat where each row is; `com.fpetrola.tui.Drawing#drawOn(Screen): void` draws a fixed thing, but the box is drawn with whichever item is selected at the time.
