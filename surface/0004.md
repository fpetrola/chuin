## Need

ShowingAScreenOnATerminalTest needs what was drawn on a Screen to reach a real console: a Terminal built over any OutputStream that clears the display and writes each line of the screen at the start of its row with ANSI cursor positioning, encoded in UTF-8 so frame characters survive. Tests pass a ByteArrayOutputStream; the demo will pass System.out.

## Considered

Screen#lines() lets a client read what was drawn, but nothing sealed writes it anywhere outside the process; Desktop#draw(Screen) only draws into memory.
