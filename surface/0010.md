## Need

TogglingACheckBoxTest needs a check box a window can hold, that Space toggles while it has the focus and that is drawn as [ ] or [X] before its text. A third Window#add overload per component would keep growing, so Button, Label and CheckBox become Views and Window#add(View, Point) is the only way to place one. Keys#space and KeyVisitor#space tell Space apart; ViewVisitor#checkBox hands the painter the text and mark, and the desktop a way to toggle the focused box, so no class grows methods of its own.

## Considered

Window#add(Button, Point) and Window#add(Label, Point) each place one kind of view; a check box would need a third overload, and every later component another. Keys#enter and Keys#tab cannot name Space. ViewVisitor#button hands over listeners to press, not a mark to toggle.

## Removes

- `com.fpetrola.tui.Window#add(Button): void`, replaced by Window#add(View, Point); PressingAButtonTest and TypingOnAKeyboardTest now place their button at point(0, 0)
- `com.fpetrola.tui.Window#add(Button, Point): void`, replaced by Window#add(View, Point)
- `com.fpetrola.tui.Window#add(Label, Point): void`, replaced by Window#add(View, Point)
- `com.fpetrola.tui.Button#accept(ViewVisitor): void`, now inherited from View#accept(ViewVisitor)
- `com.fpetrola.tui.Label#accept(ViewVisitor): void`, now inherited from View#accept(ViewVisitor)
