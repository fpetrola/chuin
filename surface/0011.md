## Grows

- tui
  - `com.fpetrola.tui.Mark interface`
  - `com.fpetrola.tui.Mark#accept(MarkVisitor): void`
  - `com.fpetrola.tui.MarkVisitor interface`
  - `com.fpetrola.tui.MarkVisitor#checked(): void`
  - `com.fpetrola.tui.MarkVisitor#unchecked(): void`
  - `com.fpetrola.tui.ViewVisitor#checkBox(CheckBox, String, Mark, Runnable): void`

## Need

ViewVisitor#checkBox handed over a boolean that made every visitor choose between two behaviours (cerco: flag-argument on the painter). The check box is still visited once, through a single ViewVisitor#checkBox, but its state is now a Mark it hands over: a visitor that cares asks the mark to accept a MarkVisitor, which is told checked or unchecked. A new box is unchecked; its toggle switches the mark.

## Considered

ViewVisitor#button(Button, String, List<ButtonListener>) hands over listeners to press, not a mark to toggle, so it cannot carry a check box. The check box member it replaces, ViewVisitor#checkBox(CheckBox, String, boolean, Runnable), passed the mark as a flag, so the painter branched on it instead of the box saying what it is.

## Removes

- `com.fpetrola.tui.ViewVisitor#checkBox(CheckBox, String, boolean, Runnable): void`, replaced by ViewVisitor#checkBox(CheckBox, String, Mark, Runnable)
