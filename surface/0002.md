## Need

Desktop#type(Key) has to reach the focused button of the top window, but Desktop#open(Window) only receives the Window interface, and neither Window nor Button can be asked to hand over what they hold without a cast or an identity lookup. A package-private ViewVisitor lets each view hand its parts (buttons, listeners) to whoever visits it, so key dispatch lives in a visitor and no class grows methods of its own. Clients cannot see ViewVisitor.

## Considered

Window#add(Button) and Button#onPress(ButtonListener) only take things in; nothing sealed lets the desktop get the buttons out of a Window or the listeners out of a Button, so the first implementation needed a Catalog looking implementations up by identity, which cerco flagged as unpromised members.
