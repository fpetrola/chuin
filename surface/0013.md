## Need

TypingIntoAnInputLineTest needs a field a window can hold that keeps the letters typed while it has the focus and is drawn as its text followed by underscores up to its width. The keyboard must tell a printable character apart from other bytes (KeyVisitor#character), and the view visitor must hand over the line's text, width and where typed characters go (ViewVisitor#inputLine), so the desktop types into the focused line and the painter draws it without casts.

## Considered

KeyVisitor#other() is what a letter turns into today, and it says nothing about which letter; ViewVisitor#label(String) shows text but nothing can be typed into it.
